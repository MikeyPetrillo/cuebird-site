🐦 Cuebird

Routines for brains that don't feel time.

Cuebird is an iOS routine timer built for neurodivergent and ADHD adults. It turns any routine into small, timed, color-coded steps — the whole screen becomes the current step's color, real alarms start your routines on schedule, and Cuebird learns how long your routines actually take.
