Do it together

Link the people you live with into a household. When someone starts a routine, everyone else gets a gentle offer to jump in, right where the run already is.

  • Join in one tap, landing on the current step instead of starting over.
  • Sitting it out is silent. Nobody is told either way.
  • As many households as you need, each with its own people and routines.

How households work

Real alarms, not notifications. A start time rings through silent mode and Focus, even with the app closed, and puts you straight into the routine.

A voice if you want one. Spoken steps and a 30-second heads-up, or a chime, or quiet. You can record a step in your own voice; recordings stay on your device.

It learns your real timing. Cuebird notices how long a morning actually takes, which steps run over, and which you skip, then updates the plan in one tap.

One-time purchase. Family Sharing covers up to six people. No subscription, no ads, no accounts, no data collection. Your routines live on your device and in your own iCloud.
