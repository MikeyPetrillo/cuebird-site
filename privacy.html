Cuebird Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

The short version

Cuebird collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. Your routines and your run history never reach us, because there is no "us" to reach. Cuebird has no server.

What Cuebird stores, and where

Cuebird saves the routines you create and a record of the routines you run (step durations, skips, completions, and your streak). This data lives in two places, both of which belong to you:

  • On your device, in the app's private storage.
  • In your own iCloud account, via Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your routines survive deleting the app or moving to a new phone, and stay in sync across your devices.
  • In a shared iCloud area, only if you join a household: routine starts you announce to your household live in an iCloud space shared between its members, run by Apple and readable only by them. See "Data shared with others" below.

iCloud sync uses your Apple Account. Your data is stored in your personal iCloud container and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer of Cuebird cannot see, access, or retrieve it. If you disable iCloud, Cuebird keeps working with on-device storage only.

Data shared with others

Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. Cuebird does not transmit your information to the developer or to any third party.

Two features move data between people, both started by you:

  • Sending a routine creates a link or file containing only that routine's steps. It travels through whatever app you send it with, exactly like text you type. Voice recordings never travel with a shared routine.
  • Households (optional): if you create or join a household, routine starts you announce are written to a shared area of your iCloud, run by Apple and readable only by the people in your household. A start includes the routine's steps, the name you chose for yourself, and the start time. Old starts are overwritten by newer ones. Nobody in the household is told whether you joined a routine, and Cuebird has no servers and cannot read any of it. You can leave any household at any time from the household screen, and household alerts are ordinary notifications that Focus and Do Not Disturb silence like anything else.

One technical note for completeness: once a day the app asks Apple's public App Store catalog whether a newer version of Cuebird exists, so it can offer you the update. The request contains nothing about you or your device, and the developer never sees it.

Permissions Cuebird asks for

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): only to ring the start times you schedule yourself, so a routine can begin on time. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice cue for a step, and only if you use that feature. Recordings are saved on your device, never uploaded, and never leave it.
  • Notifications: only for alerts you opt into, like household starts and the optional check-in after a few days away. They are ordinary notifications; Focus and Do Not Disturb silence them like anything else.

Cuebird does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, health data, or calendars.

Children

Cuebird is safe for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its on-device data. To also remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud for Cuebird in Settings → [your name] → iCloud, or delete the app's iCloud data from that screen.

For households: leaving a household (on Cuebird's household screen) removes your access to its shared area. If you created the household, deleting it removes the shared area and everything in it for everyone. Starts you announced are overwritten by newer ones either way.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because Cuebird's developer collects and holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. Your routines and history live only on your devices and in your personal iCloud account, which you control directly. Payments are processed by Apple as the merchant; refunds and the EU 14-day right of withdrawal are handled through Apple. For any privacy question, contact support@cuebird.net.

This website

cuebird.net sets no cookies, runs no analytics or trackers, and loads no third-party resources. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.
