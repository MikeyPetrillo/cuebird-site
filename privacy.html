Cuebird Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version

Cuebird collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. Your routines and your run history never reach us, because there is no "us" to reach — Cuebird has no server.

What Cuebird stores, and where

Cuebird saves the routines you create and a record of the routines you run (step durations, skips, completions, and your streak). This data lives in two places, both of which belong to you:

  • On your device, in the app's private storage.
  • In your own iCloud account, via Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your routines survive deleting the app or moving to a new phone, and stay in sync across your devices.

iCloud sync uses your Apple Account. Your data is stored in your personal iCloud container and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer of Cuebird cannot see, access, or retrieve it. If you disable iCloud, Cuebird keeps working with on-device storage only.

Data shared with others

None. Cuebird does not transmit your information to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions Cuebird asks for

  • Alarms (AlarmKit) — only to ring the start times you schedule yourself, so a routine can begin on time. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Cuebird does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, health data, or calendars.

Children

Cuebird is safe for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its on-device data. To also remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud for Cuebird in Settings → [your name] → iCloud, or delete the app's iCloud data from that screen.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
